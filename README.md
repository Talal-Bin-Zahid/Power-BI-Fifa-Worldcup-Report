### Historical Tournament Analysis (1930–2018)

## 📌 Project Overview

The **FIFA World Cup Report** is an interactive Power BI dashboard that delivers a historical analysis of the FIFA World Cup tournaments from **1930 to 2018**.
The report focuses on **competition growth, national team performance, hosting patterns, and global attendance trends**, transforming raw tournament data into meaningful, visually driven insights.

This project demonstrates strong capabilities in **data modeling, DAX calculations, ETL processes, and analytical storytelling using Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze how the World Cup has evolved over time in terms of **matches, goals, and participating teams**
* Identify **dominant football nations** based on wins, goals, and qualifications
* Evaluate **hosting frequency and geographical distribution**
* Visualize **global attendance patterns** across countries and years
